Who We Are

A new kind of
policy institution

Spectra is a lean, AI-powered research institute dedicated to producing evidence-based analysis at the intersection of international relations, data science, and policy. Based in Nairobi, we bring an African perspective to global dynamics.

We combine the analytical rigour of traditional think tanks with the speed and tools of a digital-first institution — publishing faster, working openly, and making our data available to the public.

i.

Analytical Rigour

Every output is evidence-based. Claims are sourced. Methodology is documented and transparent.

ii.

Regional Depth

East and Horn of Africa is our primary geographic lens. Global dynamics anchored in regional specificity.

iii.

Open Knowledge

Research is published publicly. Data is shared where possible. Knowledge should circulate freely.

iv.

AI-Native Practice

AI amplifies our analytical capacity — accelerating research without replacing intellectual integrity.
